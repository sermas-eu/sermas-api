I RECEIVED A NOTICE ABOUT NEW OPENING HOURS:	From 1 October the library opens at 9:00 and closes at 19:00 from Monday to Saturday.
WHAT HAPPENS TO MY RESERVATIONS FROM 1 OCTOBER:	Reservations made before 1 October stay valid; you are told by email when a book is ready.
I HAVE A JUNIOR CARD, WHY DID I NOT RECEIVE THE NOTICE:	Notices go to the parent or guardian who signed for the card.

HOW CAN I CHECK THE STATUS OF A BOOK I RESERVED:	
You can check the status of your reservation:   - from your account page, under Reservations   - at the front desk, with your card number

HOW CAN I CHECK THE STATUS OF A BOOK REQUESTED FROM ANOTHER LIBRARY:	
An inter-library loan usually takes one to two weeks; its status is shown in your account page as soon as the other library ships it.

HOW DO RETURNS AND RENEWALS WORK:	
Return books at any branch or at the return box by the entrance; a loan can be renewed twice online if nobody else has reserved the book.
