HOW DO I GET A LIBRARY CARD?	Ask at the front desk of any branch with an identity document; the card is free and ready in a few minutes.
WHAT CAN I DO WITH THE LIBRARY CARD?	With the card you can: - borrow up to ten books at a time - book a study room - use the computers and the printers.
MY CARD IS ABOUT TO EXPIRE OR HAS EXPIRED, HOW DO I RENEW IT?	Renew it at the front desk or online from your account page; the new card works as soon as it is renewed.
I AM NOT 18 YET, CAN I STILL GET A CARD?	Yes, the junior card is for readers under 18 and needs the signature of a parent or guardian.
